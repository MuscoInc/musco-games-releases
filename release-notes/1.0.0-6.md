The first Musco Games release for Android. Installing it doesn't require the Play Store.

**Install:** download `musco-games-1.0.0-6-universal.apk` below and open it. Step-by-step help, including what to tap when Android asks about unknown apps, is in the [README](https://github.com/MuscoInc/musco-games-releases#install).

**What's in it:**

- Browse tournaments and follow them live: matches, standings, brackets and players.
- Register for tournaments and track your matches.
- Host your own: create, publish, run the draw, enter results and share images.
- Includes the first app update: clearer match filters (All, By round, By team), easier-to-read text, and quick add for players.

**Details:**

- Android 7.0 or newer, about 170 MB.
- Signed by Google, the same as the Play Store version, so Google Play will update it once the app is public there.
